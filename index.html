ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A NOTE FROM YUXIN

Helping families feel more independent online.

Growing up, I often helped my parents navigate websites, online forms, accounts, and other digital tasks. Many immigrant families have similar experiences: important systems may be unfamiliar, unavailable in their preferred language, or unnecessarily difficult to understand.

I’m building Global Hub to make that process less confusing. My goal is to create multilingual, accessible guidance for people learning independently and for family members helping someone else.

This project is still growing. I plan to add translations, narrated instructions, printable guides, and video tutorials demonstrating the digital tasks I helped my own parents complete.
