IMMEDIATE DANGER?

Contact emergency services for your location.

Follow instructions from local authorities. Global Hub provides general preparation information and does not replace official emergency guidance.

SECTION 01 · START HERE

What should you do first?

If conditions are changing quickly, focus on official instructions and the safety of the people around you.

  1. 1

    Check official local alerts

    Look for updates from emergency services, local government agencies, and trusted weather organizations.

  2. 2

    Follow evacuation or shelter instructions

    Do not delay if authorities tell your area to evacuate or shelter in place.

  3. 3

    Contact your household

    Use text messages when phone networks are busy and follow your family communication plan.

  4. 4

    Avoid unsafe areas

    Do not enter damaged buildings or drive through flooded roads.

SECTION 02 · PREPARE

Prepare your household ahead of time.

Small preparations can make it easier to respond when conditions change.

i

Helpful tip: Keep a printed copy of important phone numbers in case your phone or internet connection is unavailable.

☎ 01

Create a contact plan

Choose an out-of-area contact and a safe household meeting location.

+ 02

Build an emergency kit

Include water, food, medication, flashlights, chargers, and important documents.

↗ 03

Know your exit routes

Identify more than one safe way to leave your home and neighborhood.

✓ 04

Save official sources

Bookmark local emergency, weather, and government websites before you need them.

SECTION 04 · TRUSTED INFORMATION

Always verify emergency information.

During an emergency, use local government agencies, emergency services, and recognized weather organizations as your primary sources.

Global Hub should help you understand where to begin—it should never replace instructions from authorities in your area.
