Be prepared

Essential emergency steps

Small preparations can make it easier to respond quickly when conditions change.

01

Create a contact plan

Choose an out-of-area contact and make sure everyone in your household knows how to reach them.

02

Build an emergency kit

Include water, nonperishable food, medication, flashlights, chargers, and important documents.

03

Know your exit routes

Identify multiple evacuation routes and choose a safe meeting location ahead of time.

04

Follow verified alerts

Use information from emergency services, local government agencies, and trusted weather organizations.

During an emergency

Respond carefully and stay informed

✓

Follow evacuation or shelter instructions from local authorities.

✓

Use text messages when phone networks are congested.

✓

Never enter damaged buildings or drive through flooded roads.

✓

Check on neighbors only when conditions are safe.
