WHY THIS GUIDE EXISTS

Education information should be understandable.

Applications and financial-aid systems can be difficult to navigate, especially for first-generation students and families unfamiliar with the process. This guide breaks the process into smaller steps.

SECTION 02 · FINANCIAL AID

Understand your options.

Financial aid may come from governments, schools, nonprofit organizations, community groups, or employers.

01

Grants

Financial assistance that usually does not need to be repaid. Eligibility may be based on financial need.

02

Scholarships

Awards may be based on need, academics, community service, background, location, or field of study.

03

Work-study

Eligible students may qualify for part-time employment while attending school.

04

Student loans

Borrowed money that must be repaid. Review the interest, repayment terms, and alternatives before accepting one.

OFFICIAL U.S. RESOURCE

The FAFSA form is the official application for federal financial aid for eligible college, career-school, and graduate students.

Watch for scholarship scams.
